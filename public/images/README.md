## Image Guidelines

- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Gallery Images**: 800x800px (square) works best
- **Hero Image**: 1200x800px or similar landscape orientation
- **File Size**: Optimize images to under 500KB for faster loading
- **Quality**: High-quality photos that show details of your work

## Image Optimization Tools

- [TinyPNG](https://tinypng.com/) - Compress images without losing quality
- [Squoosh](https://squoosh.app/) - Advanced image compression
- [ImageOptim](https://imageoptim.com/) - Mac app for optimization
